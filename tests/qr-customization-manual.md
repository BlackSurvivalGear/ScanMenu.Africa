# QR customization manual checks

- Classic generates a black QR on white background.
- Brand Gold generates a gold-accent QR and retains the business logo when enabled.
- Premium Branded generates a printable framed canvas with the business name above the QR, centred logo when available, and ScanMenu.Africa footer branding.
- Custom foreground and background colours are reflected in the generated QR.
- Low-contrast combinations are blocked with a warning.
- Disabling Use Business Logo removes the logo from the QR.
- Reset to Default restores Classic, black, white, and logo enabled.
- Download QR exports the currently generated style.
- Open Menu and Copy Menu Link remain in Menu Builder.
